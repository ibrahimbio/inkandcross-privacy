Ink & Cross: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Ink & Cross does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

The game works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet while you play.

The app saves a few preferences (opponent, difficulty level and whether sound is on) on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children: the app contains no ads, links or data collection, and is suitable for all ages.
